About Goal Getters

Goal Getters is an experiential agency designed to provide innovative solutions across the on-ground experiential medium.

Led by Dileep Mishra, Managing Director, the team brings more than 20 years of experience in experiential marketing, BTL, OOH, planning, media buying, implementation and vendor management.

Experience spans brands including HUL, Gulf Lubricants, ITC, Sony Entertainment Television, Tata Chemicals, Eureka Forbes, Heinz, Mondelez, Dixcy, Jeevika and BSACS.
